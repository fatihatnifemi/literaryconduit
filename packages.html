COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we start working together.

How do I choose the right package for my book? +

Start with a free discovery call. We will talk through your book, your goals, your timeline, and your budget, then recommend the package that best fits where you are now and where you want to go.

Do you offer payment plans? +

Yes. For the Growth and Bestseller packages, we offer flexible payment plans split across 2 or 3 months. Please mention this during your discovery call so we can set it up.

Can I upgrade my package later? +

Absolutely. Many authors start with the Seedling Package to build their foundation, then move up to Growth or Bestseller as their career grows. We will credit your previous investment toward the new package.

What genres do you work with? +

We work across fiction, non-fiction, memoir, poetry, children's literature, and more. If your book is written with care and intention, we would love to help it find its readers.

Do you work with traditionally published authors? +

Yes. We work with authors across all publishing paths, including traditional, hybrid, and independent. Our strategies adapt to your situation.

How long does it take to see results? +

Book marketing is a long game. Some results, like improved Amazon conversions, can show within weeks. Others, like building a committed readership, take months and sometimes years. We set realistic expectations upfront and track progress together.

Still Not Sure? Let's Talk.

A free, no-pressure discovery call is the best way to figure out what your book actually needs. We will listen, offer honest insight, and help you decide, whether or not you choose to work with us.
