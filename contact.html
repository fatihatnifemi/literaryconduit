COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Is the discovery call really free? +

Yes, completely free and no obligation. The discovery call is a chance for us to learn about your book and for you to learn about our approach. Even if you decide not to work with us, you will walk away with valuable insight.

Do you work with authors outside the United States? +

Absolutely. While we are based in New York City, we work with authors across the globe. All of our services are delivered remotely, and we are experienced in collaborating across time zones.

My book is not finished yet. Can I still reach out? +

Yes, and we encourage it. The earlier you start thinking about marketing, the better your launch will be. Many of our most successful clients began working with us during the writing or editing phase.

How quickly will I hear back from you? +

We respond to all inquiries within 48 business hours. If you reach out on a Friday evening, expect to hear from us by Monday morning at the latest.
