The Original Description

AFTER

What begins as a run from her shattered life becomes something she never expected: a road trip across the country, a stranger who sees her clearly for the first time, and the one truth she spent twenty years trying to forget. For readers of Taylor Jenkins Reid and Celeste Ng, this is a story about the brave, beautiful mess of starting over."
