OUR STORY

The Conduit Between Authors and Their Readers

A boutique book marketing studio built on literary love, strategic precision, and an unwavering belief that every great book deserves to be found.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Fatihat Nifemi

Founder & Literary Marketing Strategist

Fatihat Nifemi is a Book Marketer and Author Growth Strategist with a deep love for literature and an equally deep belief in the power of intentional marketing to change an author's career.

Based in New York City and working with authors worldwide, Fatihat founded Fatihat Nifemi Literary Conduit as a response to a quiet frustration she kept seeing in publishing: beautiful books, passionate authors, and brilliant stories that never reached the readers who would have loved them most.

Her approach is rooted in the understanding that marketing a book is never just about selling copies. It is about building a bridge, a conduit, between the author's vision and the reader's hunger. Every strategy she designs is bespoke, thoughtful, and committed to honoring both sides of that bridge.

OUR JOURNEY

Why We Exist

The story behind Fatihat Nifemi Literary Conduit and the authors we serve.

Fatihat Nifemi Literary Conduit was born from a simple observation and a stubborn conviction. The observation: that too many extraordinary books go unseen, not because they lack merit, but because they lack visibility. The conviction: that this is a solvable problem.

In a publishing world where authors are increasingly expected to be their own marketers, publicists, and brand managers, we exist to carry that weight alongside them. We are the strategic partner authors wish they had sooner, the ones who understand both the craft and the commerce, the story and the strategy.

"Every book has a reader waiting for it. Our work is to make sure they find each other."

From our studio base in New York City, we work with authors across genres, geographies, and career stages. Debut novelists building their first readership. Established authors scaling their platforms. Non-fiction experts turning their books into movements. What unites them is a commitment to their work and a readiness for their work to be seen.

We are small by design. Boutique by philosophy. Personal by promise. Because the authors we serve deserve more than a transaction. They deserve a true literary conduit.

OUR APPROACH

How We Think About Book Marketing

Our philosophy shapes everything we do, from the first discovery call to the final campaign review.

01

Strategy Before Tactics

We never start with a to-do list. We start with questions. Who is this book for? What does success look like? What is the long game? Only then do we build the plan.

02

Voice Above All

Every author has a distinctive voice. Our job is to amplify it, never to flatten it into something generic. Your marketing should sound like you, not like us.

03

Readers Are People

We never think of readers as traffic, leads, or conversions. They are people with limited time, specific tastes, and real emotional lives. We market with that respect at the center.

04

Patience Is Strategy

Great author careers are built over years, not weeks. We design strategies that compound over time, not ones that burn bright and fade fast.

05

Data Serves Story

We use data, metrics, and analytics rigorously. But data is a tool, not a boss. The story of your book and your career always leads.

06

Craft Deserves Respect

You spent months or years writing this book. We treat that time with the reverence it deserves, bringing the same care to your marketing that you brought to your pages.
